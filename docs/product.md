# Product Notes

## Product

SDE 2030 Blueprint Explorer is an independent public-interest interface for the Sarawak Digital Economy Blueprint 2030.

The complete Blueprint baseline is the current product. Implementation evidence is intentionally deferred until coverage and maintenance capacity are broad enough for a separate tracking function.

## Audience

- Sarawakians looking for a clear overview of the Blueprint
- researchers, journalists, policy watchers, and civic readers
- the maintainer, who needs a structured baseline for later evidence-backed updates

## Stage 1 experience

- scan-first summary metrics
- a horizontally scrollable pillar filter, compact roadmap-phase picker, and text search aligned on one row at desktop widths and stacked cleanly on mobile
- expandable initiative cards
- five colour-coded, display-only pillar classification pills in the same card-header role as the PCDS tracker's Sector and Enabler pills
- original objectives, descriptions, intended outcomes, targets, timelines, and lead agencies
- English interface routes, with previous BM entry points temporarily serving the English experience
- baseline and methodology route explaining the evidence boundary
- static pre-rendering, direct routes, theme control, and responsive layouts
- route-specific search metadata, reciprocal English/BM language alternates, structured data, and a validated sitemap

## Deferred implementation research

- the existing evidence pilot remains stored locally and validated
- no evidence statuses, observations, source links or pilot metrics appear in the current explorer
- future implementation tracking should launch as a clearly separate function with sustainable coverage
- no delivery percentage or milestone completion ratio is calculated from Blueprint commitments

## Product principles

- Preserve the Blueprint's published hierarchy and wording materially.
- Keep baseline commitments separate from current evidence.
- Never infer completion or failure from a passed timeline.
- Avoid visual or textual cues that imply government affiliation.
- Keep the interface useful before attempting comprehensive live-status research.

## Planned stages

1. Complete Blueprint Explorer with all 106 initiatives.
2. Refine the baseline explorer and its Blueprint navigation.
3. Restore the BM experience after its reader-facing copy and route metadata have been reviewed.
4. Consider a separate implementation tracker when reliable sources and maintenance capacity allow.
